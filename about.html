The show and the series

One signal.
Different ways to learn.

The Product Signal is the parent podcast. Our earlier series explore product fundamentals and the transition into a PM career, available on YouTube and podcast platforms.

Build the Signal is our flagship hands-on series—and our third series overall. In its first season, we take real product questions through the ambiguity, strategy, prototype, and critique.

New full-length builds arrive on YouTube on the first Tuesday of every month. Applied practice comes first; interview preparation is another way into the work.

Your hosts

Meet Brad & Deb.

Brad Eiben

Co-host · Product leader & educator

From product launches and lean projects at Toyota to leadership roles at MSA and beyond; Brad has spent 20+ years building, coaching, and directing teams across industries, engineering disciplines, and corporate cultures. He now serves as Executive Director of Carnegie Mellon's MS in Product Management program. A servant-leader by nature, his focus has always been the same: helping people solve the right problem.

Our approach

Product judgement grows through practice.

Make the thinking visible.

Explain the customer, the assumptions, and the options we leave behind. A framework earns its place by helping us make a decision.

Learn through building.

A prototype gives us something specific to question. The gaps, revisions, and tradeoffs are part of the lesson.

Learn with each other.

Bring your perspective to the next challenge. Share your decisions, question ours, and show us what building revealed. The discussion is part of the practice.
